## Quick Answer

The official October 5, 2026 content announcement schedules an October 27 update with free and paid content. **Announced; release not yet confirmed** in the official sources checked at 23:28 UTC on October 26, 2026 (October 27 in China). The calendar date alone does not confirm availability in US regions or on every platform. The free side adds [Tower Farm](/locations/tower-farm), features, fixes, and quality-of-life improvements. Paid content adds two Michael skins, a Michelle and Eugene Civilian Characters Pack, and Civilian Costumes Pack 1. The latest confirmed patch is [1.1.1, released October 16](/guides/patch-notes).

## Free content

- **Tower Farm** is a free multiplayer map for all players. Its confirmed setting and landmarks are on the [Tower Farm page](/locations/tower-farm).
- **Scythe** is announced as a free weapon found on Tower Farm and unlockable through in-game challenges. Purchasing the Halloween 5 Michael skin unlocks it immediately; the announcement does not specify the challenge requirements.
- **Features, fixes, and QoL** are announced alongside the map, without an itemized change list.
