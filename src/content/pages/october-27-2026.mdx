## Quick Answer

The official October 5, 2026 content announcement schedules an October 27 update with free and paid content. **It is scheduled, not live** as of this page's October 6 update date. The free side adds [Tower Farm](/locations/tower-farm), features, fixes, and quality-of-life improvements. Paid content adds two Michael skins, a Michelle and Eugene Civilian Characters Pack, and Civilian Costumes Pack 1. The current live patch remains [Patch 1.1.0](/updates/patch-1-1-0).

## Free content

- **Tower Farm** is a free multiplayer map for all players. Its confirmed setting and landmarks are on the [Tower Farm page](/locations/tower-farm).
- **Features, fixes, and QoL** are announced alongside the map, without an itemized change list.

## Paid DLC

| Item | Type | Announced US price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Halloween 5 Michael skin | Michael cosmetic | $9.99 USD |
| Halloween 2018 Michael skin | Michael cosmetic | $9.99 USD |
| Michelle + Eugene Civilian Characters Pack | Two playable Civilians, sold together | $9.99 USD |
| Civilian Costumes Pack 1 | 14 outfits with 42 color tints | $9.99 USD |

The Michael skins and the Michelle and Eugene pack are separate purchases; the character pack is not split into standalone characters. See [Michael's upcoming skins](/characters/michael-myers) and the [character roster](/characters) for the existing lineup.

## What is not established yet

The announcement does not give Tower Farm statistics, abilities, perks, exact release times, or skin bonuses. Those details will be added only after the update is live and officially documented.
