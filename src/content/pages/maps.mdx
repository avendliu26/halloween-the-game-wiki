Halloween: The Game has four announced launch multiplayer maps: East Haddonfield, Haddonfield Heights, Orange Grove Estates and Haddonfield Town Center. Each covers a different part of the town, mixing recognizable film locations with areas created for the game.

The [official September 1 map overview](https://halloweengame.com/news/the-locations-of-halloween-the-game/) establishes the launch list. This directory compares those locations; the entries below provide individual landmark references, not verified spawn charts or solved escape routes.

## The four launch maps

| Map | Character of the area | Named landmarks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [East Haddonfield](/locations/east-haddonfield) | Rural outskirts and agricultural businesses | Rabbit in Red Lounge, Phelps Garage, Midwest Feed Company |
| [Haddonfield Heights](/locations/haddonfield-heights) | Modest residential neighborhood | Myers house, Strode house, Lampkin Lane, water tower |
| [Orange Grove Estates](/locations/orange-grove-estates) | Larger multi-story homes and neighborhood park | Doyle and Wallace residences |
| [Haddonfield Town Center](/locations/haddonfield-town-center) | Downtown shops and gathering places | Nichols Hardware, A-Side Music Store, Hill Garden Center, Patty's Pub |

These names are map and landmark names, not four difficulty levels. The official overview does not rank the neighborhoods by escape rate, balance or suitability for new players.

## Landmarks, not fixed escape routes

The [multiplayer overview](https://halloweengame.com/news/multiplayer-gameplay-overview/) describes points of interest containing NPC residents, loot, telephones, hiding places and escape opportunities. It also says escape opportunities and the items needed to use them change between matches.

That distinction matters when reading any map reference. Knowing that a building exists does not establish that a key item always appears there, that the same vehicle is always usable or that the same route wins every time. This wiki does not turn a scenic flythrough into a fixed objective walkthrough.

## What distinguishes the neighborhoods?

East Haddonfield gives the launch set a rural area rather than another street of family homes. Its bar, garage and feed-company setting distinguish it from the domestic landmarks associated with the film. Use the named businesses as orientation references without assuming unannounced services or interactions.

Haddonfield Heights places the Myers and Strode homes within a smaller residential neighborhood. The water tower and Lampkin Lane are confirmed landmarks. The overview establishes their presence, not a precise scale, grid coordinate or collectible route.

Orange Grove Estates has the richest dedicated layout description among the sources reviewed here. The [official Orange Grove reveal](https://halloweengame.com/news/orange-grove-estates/) describes upper-floor interiors, balconies, yards, fences and hedges, alongside a park with playground equipment and winding paths. It also describes multiple entrances and windows at familiar houses, repaired vehicles and bicycles.

Town Center shifts the setting to businesses and public gathering places. Nichols Hardware is one of its recognizable anchors, with a music store, garden center and pub also named. A shop's narrative purpose does not prove a particular loot table.

## How maps connect to multiplayer objectives

Civilians do more than look for their own exit: they locate and warn residents and help them reach escape opportunities. Michael can interfere with the town's lighting and exploit stealth. Read the [gameplay overview](/guides/how-to-play) for those systems before treating scenery as strategy.

For Orange Grove specifically, the developer describes fences, hedges and multi-level houses as meaningful navigation obstacles. That supports expecting different sightlines and routes through the neighborhood. It does not support claiming a tested safe loop, an invulnerable hiding place or a guaranteed escape sequence.

## Are these all the singleplayer locations?

The cited September announcement explicitly describes the four multiplayer launch maps. It should not be treated as an exhaustive chapter-by-chapter singleplayer location list. Story chapters and multiplayer maps serve different descriptions in the official material.

No future map schedule, exact map dimensions or verified loot coordinates are included in the sources used for this directory. Those details should be added only when supported by further documentation or clearly identified hands-on evidence.
