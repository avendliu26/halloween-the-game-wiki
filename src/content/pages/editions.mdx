Halloween: The Game Digital Standard costs $39.99 USD and Digital Deluxe costs $59.99 USD in the reviewed US listings. Deluxe adds Richard and Alexis, the Inmate Michael Myers skin, a Laurie cosmetic, and a digital comic. Early Access and the Phantom skin were pre-order benefits, not current reasons to choose an edition.

The [official digital pre-order FAQ](https://halloweengame.com/news/preorder/) is the source for the contents below. The [US PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10014718/) currently agrees with those two US prices. Local currency prices and store checkout terms can differ.

## Standard versus Digital Deluxe

| Included item | Digital Standard | Digital Deluxe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base game | Yes | Yes |
| Inmate Michael Myers skin | Not listed | Included |
| Richard and Alexis Civilian characters | Not listed | Included |
| Advance access to Final Girl Laurie Strode cosmetic | Not listed | Included |
| Halloween: The Game comic | Not listed | Included |
| Phantom Michael Myers skin | Digital pre-order bonus | Digital pre-order bonus |
| Early gameplay access from September 4 | Not listed | Deluxe pre-order benefit |

The $20 USD difference follows from the announced base prices, not a temporary discount. This comparison concerns digital products; a physical Collector's Edition is a separate package rather than another name for Digital Deluxe.

## Which benefits depend on pre-ordering?

The Phantom Michael Myers skin was a digital pre-order reward for both editions. According to the official FAQ, it would not be sold after the pre-order period. That statement is narrower than saying every Deluxe extra disappeared at launch.

Early gameplay access started September 4, 2026, at 9 AM PT for eligible Digital Deluxe pre-orders. The general digital release followed on September 8. Standard pre-orders did not qualify for the Deluxe early-access window.

Inmate Michael Myers, Richard and Alexis are Deluxe contents rather than Standard pre-order extras. Keep edition ownership separate from expired pre-order entitlements.

## Characters and cosmetics are different extras

Richard and Alexis are described as exclusive playable Civilians. Final Girl is a cosmetic for Laurie Strode, not an additional character slot. Inmate and Phantom are Michael Myers skins, not separately confirmed killers with new ability kits.

For the base roster and the distinction between playable Civilians and town residents, use the [character directory](/characters). This comparison makes no claim that an edition-exclusive character is stronger, easier or required to succeed. The reviewed purchase descriptions do not provide comparative character statistics.

The announcement also lists a game comic. Its exact delivery format and the contents of unspecified “surprises” are not established by the reviewed FAQ, so they are not expanded into invented items here.

## Can Standard owners upgrade later?

The publisher announced a Digital Deluxe Edition Upgrade for launch, including the two exclusive Civilians and the Inmate skin. The reviewed FAQ does not establish its standalone price or separate individual-character prices, so check the live store for current upgrade availability.

The upgrade announcement is not evidence that the Phantom pre-order skin became purchasable later. The same FAQ explicitly treats Phantom differently; check the live upgrade listing rather than assuming every expired pre-order entitlement is included.

## Is this the same as the Collector's Edition?

No. The [physical-edition FAQ](https://halloweengame.com/news/physical-editions/) lists different packages, cosmetics and physical goods with an October 6 release. It also says physical pre-orders do not qualify for the digital-exclusive Phantom skin.

Choose between digital and disc based on access date, hardware and the extras you actually want. The [physical comparison](/physical-editions) explains those packages without assuming that Digital Deluxe contents carry over.
