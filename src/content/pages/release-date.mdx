Halloween: The Game launches digitally on September 8, 2026. Digital Deluxe pre-orders offer early access from September 4 at 9 AM Pacific Time, while physical editions are scheduled for October 6.

These dates describe three different purchase arrangements, not competing release announcements. The latest [official launch-map announcement](https://halloweengame.com/news/the-locations-of-halloween-the-game/) reconfirms the September digital schedule; the separate [physical-edition FAQ](https://halloweengame.com/news/physical-editions/) supplies the October disc date.

## Release schedule at a glance

| Access type | Announced date | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital Deluxe pre-order early access | September 4, 2026, 9 AM PT | Eligible digital Deluxe pre-orders |
| Digital launch | September 8, 2026 | PS5, Xbox Series X\|S, Steam and Epic in supported regions |
| Physical launch | October 6, 2026 | Announced PS5 and Xbox Series X disc editions |

The [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3219630/Halloween_The_Game/) and [US PlayStation listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/concept/10014718/) also show September 8. Store dates and availability can be regional: this table is the announced schedule, not a promise that every version is sold in every country.

## Who can play on September 4?

The [digital pre-order FAQ](https://halloweengame.com/news/preorder/) assigns early gameplay access to Digital Deluxe pre-orders and specifies 9 AM PT on Friday, September 4. A Standard digital pre-order has a cosmetic bonus, but the FAQ does not give it the same early gameplay benefit.

Check the edition name in your store receipt rather than relying on the word “pre-order.” The distinction matters because both digital versions can be pre-ordered, while only one lists early gameplay access. Our [digital edition comparison](/editions) separates permanent edition contents from time-limited pre-order rewards.

The publisher calls this early access to the release game. Do not confuse the phrase with a separately announced public beta, free trial or long-term unfinished-game development program; none of those is the entitlement described in this FAQ.

## Why is the physical copy later?

October 6 is the date in the physical-edition announcement, covering both Standard and Limited Collector's packages. The announcement does not replace September 8 for digital copies. It describes a later disc release with a different set of extras.

There is another easy naming trap: physical packages advertise advance access to certain cosmetics. The physical FAQ explains that these items are unlocked immediately for owners and can also be earned through challenges. Cosmetic advance access is not the same as the September 4 early gameplay entitlement. See [physical copies and collector packages](/physical-editions) before choosing a disc for an early start.

## What time does the Standard Edition unlock?

The sources reviewed specify an exact time for Deluxe early access, but do not establish one universal September 8 unlock hour across all stores and regions. Use the release display attached to your own store purchase for local timing.

We are not publishing a speculative countdown or an unverified preload schedule. A date shown in one country's store should not be converted into a guaranteed worldwide unlock without an official time and time-zone statement.
