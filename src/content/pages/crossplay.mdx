## Quick Answer

Yes. Halloween: The Game supports crossplay across its current PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC versions on Steam and Epic Games Store. Cross-platform parties and matchmaking are part of the released game. Patch 1.0.1 fixed an Early Access issue that sent crossplay party members back to the main menu instead of following their leader into a matchmade lobby.

Crossplay is not cross-progression. IllFonic has not published a system for carrying unlocks, purchases, or progress between PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, and Epic accounts.

## PS5, Xbox, and PC Crossplay

The released platform pool covers PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Steam, and Epic. That means the supported combinations include PS5 with Xbox, PS5 with PC, Xbox with PC, and Steam with Epic.

Every player needs the current game version. Console certification can cause a patch to arrive at a different time from PC, and mismatched builds may temporarily interrupt parties even though the platforms are normally compatible.

## How to Play with Cross-Platform Friends

1. Install every available game update on each system.
2. Open the in-game Social or Friends interface rather than relying only on the platform's native friend list.
3. Exchange or enter the in-game friend identifier shown by the current build.
4. Invite everyone to one party and let the party leader start matchmaking.
5. Confirm that each member follows the leader into the matchmade lobby.
6. If one member returns to the main menu, recreate the party after checking versions and service status.

Patch 1.0.1 specifically corrected step five: party members should now follow their leader instead of being kicked or dropped. Players still reporting failures should verify the installed version before using older launch workarounds.

## Patch 1.0.1 Crossplay Fix

IllFonic's [official launch post](https://halloweengame.com/news/halloween-the-game-out-now/) states that Patch 1.0.1 addressed the cross-play matchmaking issue experienced during Early Access. The fix makes crossplay party members follow their leader into a matchmade lobby.

This is a concrete post-launch correction, not a promise that platform services, private connections, or every account setting can never cause another failure. It also does not establish cross-progression.

## How to Turn Off Crossplay

On console, open **Settings**, move to the online/gameplay options tab, locate the **Crossplay** or **Cross-Platform Play** setting, switch it off, and apply the change before matchmaking. Current console players report the toggle in Settings, commonly on the second tab, but IllFonic has not published an official platform-by-platform menu path.

If the option does not appear, check that you are at the main menu rather than already in a lobby, then install the latest update and reopen Settings. Do not use a menu path copied from another IllFonic game.

Turning crossplay off limits matchmaking to your own platform pool. That may reduce exposure to platform-specific issues, but it can also produce longer waits and prevents you from joining friends on other systems. PC availability and the exact wording of the toggle remain insufficiently documented, so follow the options shown in your current build.

## Cross-Progression and Cross-Save

Halloween: The Game cross-progression is not officially confirmed between PS5, Xbox, Steam, and Epic. Steam Cloud can synchronize supported save data inside Steam, but it does not prove that account level, challenge progress, cosmetics, or purchases transfer to a console or another PC store.

Until IllFonic publishes an account-linking policy, assume progress and purchases remain with the platform where they were earned. Buy an edition on the system where you expect to play most often.

## Crossplay Voice Chat

Cross-platform matchmaking and voice chat are separate features. Current players report hearing other platforms when everyone uses in-game chat, but the official launch material does not publish a detailed cross-platform voice troubleshooting guide.

If party members can join but cannot hear one another, check the in-game voice settings, platform privacy permissions, microphone input, mute state, and whether anyone is using a closed console party-chat channel.

## Crossplay Troubleshooting

Try these checks in order:

1. Confirm all players are on Patch 1.0.1 or a newer current version.
2. Disband and recreate the in-game party with a different leader.
3. Verify that every player can enter online matchmaking alone.
4. Check PlayStation Network, Xbox Network, Steam, or Epic service status for the affected platform.
5. Restart the game after changing crossplay or privacy settings.
6. Test a private match to separate party formation from public matchmaking.
7. Use the [backend authentication guide](/guides/backend-authentication-error) if the error mentions login, region selection, or service authentication.

Avoid changing router ports or reinstalling immediately when several platforms report the same issue. A shared service or version mismatch cannot be fixed by destructive local troubleshooting.

## Common Questions

### Is Halloween: The Game cross platform between PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. PS5 and Xbox Series X|S are part of the released crossplay pool.

### Can Xbox and PC play together?

Yes. Xbox, Steam, and Epic players can use cross-platform parties and matchmaking when all players are on compatible current versions.

### Does Halloween: The Game have cross progression?

No cross-progression system is officially confirmed. Do not treat cloud saves on one store as proof of transfers to another platform.

### Why is my crossplay party being separated?

Install the latest build first. Patch 1.0.1 fixed a party-follow problem, but a version mismatch, platform outage, account permission, or newer service issue can produce similar symptoms.
