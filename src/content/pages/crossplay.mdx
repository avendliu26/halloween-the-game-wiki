## Quick Answer

Yes. Halloween: The Game supports crossplay on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and Windows PC through Steam and Epic Games Store. Cross-platform party members can follow their leader into matchmaking. The Early Access party-separation issue was fixed in Patch 1.0.1 on September 8.

Cross-save and cross-progression are separate features: the official sources reviewed do not confirm transferring progress or purchases between these platforms.

## Cross-Platform Parties and Matchmaking

Once your friends are in the same party, the leader starts matchmaking and the party should enter the lobby together. That is the behavior documented in the official launch patch.

Use the invitation options shown by your current game client. The reviewed official material does not document a universal friend-code format, required third-party account link, or platform-by-platform invitation menu, so an exact setup sequence is not established here.

## Patch 1.0.1 Crossplay Fix

During Early Access, cross-platform party members could be removed from the party or returned to the main menu when the leader joined matchmaking. The [September 8 launch notes](https://halloweengame.com/news/halloween-the-game-out-now/) say 1.0.1 corrected that behavior.

Treat those older failures as historical when checking compatibility. A new party failure still needs its own diagnosis; the fix does not establish a permanent service-status guarantee.

As of September 16, the latest patch listed on the [official news page](https://halloweengame.com/news/) is **1.0.2, released September 11**. Its [notes](https://halloweengame.com/news/patch-notes-1-0-2/) do not announce a change to cross-platform compatibility.

## Cross-Progression and Cross-Save

Crossplay lets players on different systems share multiplayer sessions. Cross-progression would let an account carry levels or unlocks between systems, while cross-save concerns transferring saved progress.

The reviewed official patch notes and storefronts do not establish a cross-progression or cross-save system between PlayStation, Xbox, Steam, and Epic. Steam Cloud support alone does not demonstrate transfers to consoles or another PC store. Check for an explicit transfer policy before buying a second platform copy for your existing progress.

## How to Turn Off Crossplay

Earlier console player reports described a Crossplay option in Settings. An exact menu path and availability on every platform have not been verified from official documentation. If your current client exposes that option, follow its displayed instructions.

Disabling crossplay narrows the available matchmaking pool and can prevent joining friends on other platforms. If the option is absent, the official sources reviewed do not provide a confirmed workaround.

## Crossplay Troubleshooting

These are basic checks to identify where joining fails; they are not developer-certified fixes for every connection problem.

1. **Check updates on every device.** Use the latest available build and relaunch after installing it.
2. **Check solo matchmaking.** If a player cannot join alone, investigate that player's online access before repeatedly rebuilding a mixed-platform party.
3. **Check platform services and access.** Review service status, online subscriptions where required, and multiplayer permissions.
4. **Retry the party after restarting.** Record whether the failure occurs during invitation, party formation, or the leader entering matchmaking.
5. **Report a repeatable failure.** Include all platforms, game versions, error text, and the point at which members are separated when contacting [official support](https://halloweengame.com/support-and-socials/).

If an error names backend authentication, use the [authentication troubleshooting guide](/guides/backend-authentication-error).

### What if matchmaking works but voice chat does not?

Check the voice options available in the game, microphone selection, mute state, and platform communication permissions. Being able to join a mixed-platform match does not identify the cause of an audio problem.

## Common Questions

### Is Halloween: The Game cross platform between PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. PS5 and Xbox Series X|S participate in the game's console and PC crossplay support.

### Can Xbox and PC play together?

Yes. Crossplay covers Xbox Series X|S and the Windows PC versions on Steam and Epic Games Store.

### Does Halloween: The Game have cross progression?

Cross-progression between the supported platforms is not confirmed in the official sources reviewed. Multiplayer compatibility does not establish shared unlocks or purchases.

### Why does an old guide say crossplay parties do not work?

Early Access had a party-follow problem. Patch 1.0.1 fixed that issue on September 8; current failures should be reported with the installed version and exact symptom.
