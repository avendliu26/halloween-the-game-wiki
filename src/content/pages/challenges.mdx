Halloween: The Game has three challenge systems. Use Passive Challenges for role-wide progress, Progressive Challenges for a specific character, Michael skin, or weapon, and Story Challenges for optional chapter objectives. Match your activity to the challenge type before starting another attempt.

## Passive, Progressive, or Story?

| Challenge type | How progress works | Where to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passive | Automatically tracks Killer or Civilian activity across character choices | Check the role category and its requirement |
| Progressive | Unlocks rewards through successive objectives for one Civilian, Michael skin, or weapon type | Check the active step and correct loadout |
| Story | Optional objectives in the Prologue and Chapters 1–5 | Choose a chapter below and read its exact condition |

These categories are confirmed in the [official progression overview](https://halloweengame.com/news/progression-customization-overview/). Completing a chapter alone does not satisfy every Stalk, target, weapon, location, or execution requirement.

## Progressive Requirements After 1.0.3

[Patch 1.0.3](https://halloweengame.com/news/patch-notes-1-0-3/), released September 22, rebalanced many Progressive goals. Selected examples:

| Track / challenge | Updated goal |
| --- | --- |
| Eric — Shop Class Alumni | Repair 10 cars |
| Tanya — Entourage | Escort 10 Residents to an escape as followers |
| Rachel — From the Shadows | Leave a hiding spot and stumble Michael 7 times |
| Marcus — Tinker | Repair communications equipment Michael destroyed 5 times |
| Laurie — Resourceful | Search 100 loot containers |
| 1978 Michael — Stalker Boy | Reach Stalk Tier 2 on 50 victims |
| Hatchet — Flight of the Axe | Get 75 thrown-weapon kills |

This is a selection, not the full challenge book. If your existing progress already exceeds a reduced goal, gain progress toward that objective once more to trigger completion.

## Passive Challenges After Prestige

The same patch keeps Passive progress tracking after Prestige even when the entries look locked. IllFonic calls this a temporary fix; the locked appearance remains a separate UI issue. Check progress after a qualifying action before treating the icon as a lost unlock. Full Prestige reset rules and level caps are not documented in these notes.

## Stalk, Bloodthirst, and Executions

Read the target's required Stalk condition before grabbing. If the objective asks for a specific execution, ordinary melee damage is not a substitute. Approach the required environmental object with the victim alive and check the available interaction rather than assuming a nearby kill will count.

Stalk and Bloodthirst are not interchangeable labels. A fully stalked victim does not by itself prove that Michael meets a Bloodthirst-dependent execution condition. For the relevant mechanics, see [Michael Myers](/characters/michael-myers). On no-alert attempts, also consider witnesses and discovered bodies: the target being unaware at the instant of a grab is only one part of the route.

Practical preparation:

1. Record the exact objective and chapter number.
2. Separate noisy kill-all attempts from stealth attempts until you understand the patrols.
3. Identify the target, weapon, and execution location before committing.
4. Finish the chapter and compare the saved challenge menu with any in-run notification.
5. If progress disappears, investigate tracking before repeating a long route unchanged.

## What Challenges Unlock

Story objectives award alternate outfits and cosmetic items; completing every singleplayer challenge awards a special Michael look. They are distinct from the account and character levels used for roster and loadout progression. See [character unlocks](/guides/how-to-unlock-characters).

Passive challenges track broader role activity. Progressive challenges follow a sequence tied to a Civilian, Michael skin, or weapon. Select the correct entry before assuming a story kill should advance a different challenge book.
