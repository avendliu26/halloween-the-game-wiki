Halloween: The Game lists Windows 11 64-bit, 16 GB RAM, DirectX 12 and 45 GB of SSD/NVME storage as minimum PC requirements. Steam and Epic currently publish matching core specification tables.

The CPU rows are unusually broad socket/platform labels rather than specific processor models. Treat the table as the publisher's published requirements, not as a tested performance guarantee for every machine that matches one label.

## Minimum versus recommended specifications

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Operating system | Windows 11 64-bit | Windows 11 64-bit |
| CPU label as published | Intel LGA 1200+ / AMD AM4+ | Intel LGA 1700+ / AMD AM5+ |
| Memory | 16 GB RAM | 32 GB RAM |
| Graphics | GTX 1660+ / RX 590+ / Arc A770+ | RTX 3000 series / RX 7000 series |
| Graphics target shown | 1080p at 30 fps | 4K at 30 fps |
| DirectX | Version 12 | Version 12 |
| Storage | 45 GB available; SSD/NVME required | 45 GB available; SSD/NVME required |
| Network on Steam | Broadband Internet connection | Broadband Internet connection |

Source: [Steam requirements](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3219630/Halloween_The_Game/) and [Epic requirements](https://store.epicgames.com/p/halloween-f3e2dd?lang=en-US), checked September 3, 2026. Steam also expressly requires a 64-bit processor and operating system. The GPU names and targets above retain the scope of the storefront wording.

## How to read the CPU entries

LGA 1200, LGA 1700, AM4 and AM5 identify CPU platforms or sockets. They do not identify one exact processor's speed, core count or performance. A buyer cannot reliably turn this row into an exact minimum Core i5 or Ryzen model without further publisher clarification.

The plus signs are part of the published labels. This wiki does not interpret them as a tested guarantee for every compatible processor or replace the entries with a guessed model. If your purchasing decision depends on a borderline CPU, the unresolved specificity matters more than simply checking a matching socket name.

## Do the GPU targets guarantee those frame rates?

No independent benchmark is provided here. The stores associate the listed minimum graphics options with 1080p at 30 fps and the recommended families with 4K at 30 fps, but these are published targets rather than measurements from this wiki.

The recommended row spans whole GPU families without identifying one card. It also does not specify a verified universal graphics preset in the evidence reviewed. We therefore do not extrapolate a 60 fps requirement, laptop-GPU equivalence, ray-tracing requirement or performance result for a particular PC.

## Storage, downloads and online requirements

The published storage figure is 45 GB of available space and explicitly calls for SSD/NVME storage. Available disk space is not necessarily the size of the download. Preloads, compressed downloads and subsequent patches should be checked separately in your store client.

Steam also lists broadband Internet in both columns. That table alone does not establish how every singleplayer session behaves offline. It should not be expanded into an undocumented always-online rule or an offline guarantee.

## Operating systems and controls

The reviewed PC listings specify Windows 11 64-bit. Steam's platform data lists Windows, not native macOS or Linux support. Steam Deck compatibility and compatibility-layer performance have not been verified for this article.

Steam lists full controller support, including DualShock and DualSense support in its application data. This does not substitute for a verified control-binding reference, nor does it guarantee console-specific controller effects on every PC setup.

## Check your machine before choosing a store

Compare your actual OS version, RAM, GPU model and available SSD space with the table. Record the full CPU model as well as its platform, because the official socket-only entry leaves an important gap. Recheck the live store before buying hardware or the game; the published requirements may change around launch.
