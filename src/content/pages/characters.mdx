Halloween: The Game has one playable killer, ten Standard Edition Civilians, and two additional Digital Deluxe Civilians. Michael Myers hunts the town; four Civilian players warn and rescue NPC residents, call police, prepare escapes, and fight back long enough to survive.

![Five Civilian characters standing together in an official Haddonfield cast image](/images/official/heroes-of-haddonfield.webp)

Civilian choice now affects gameplay through four stat categories, signature traits, starting-item unlocks, and a customizable Perk Deck. Use the table below as a post-launch roster reference; exact values are included only where the current evidence is strong enough.

## Civilian Stats Explained

![Civilian customization screen with character stats and loadout options](/images/official/po-customization.webp)

Every Civilian uses four official stat categories:

| Stat | What it affects |
| --- | --- |
| Athleticism | Movement on foot and by bicycle, including speed and efficiency |
| Personality | Convincing residents, alerting authorities, follower capacity, Fear resistance, and Stalk resistance |
| Resourcefulness | Repairs, environmental awareness, vehicles, and firearms |
| Capability | Vigor plus effectiveness with melee weapons and throwable household items |

No stat does every job. A fast scout may reach residents and objectives quickly, while a Personality-focused character can contribute more directly to warning and commanding residents. Resourcefulness supports escape work; Capability supports direct resistance.

The character-select screen is the best source for current numerical bars because balance can change. This page does not convert launch-week screenshots into permanent exact ratings without current build verification.

## September 22 Stat Rebalance

[Patch 1.0.3](https://halloweengame.com/news/patch-notes-1-0-3/) reduced high-Capability melee damage while raising the low end, and reduced high-Capability contribution to Struggle. Athleticism movement/sprint scaling was similarly reduced at the high end and raised at the low end. Heavyweight and Drunk melee benefits were also reduced. These changes make launch-era comparisons unreliable; exact replacement multipliers are not published.

[Patch 1.0.4](https://halloweengame.com/news/patch-notes-1-0-4/) then corrected unintended grab difficulty when Michael reaches Bloodthirst 3. See [how to struggle free](/guides/how-to-struggle-free), not an unsupported guarantee that one stat or character always escapes. These official balance changes were checked September 27; older community unlock observations retain their original evidence limits.

## Traits, Perks, and Starting Items Are Different

Signature traits belong to the selected Civilian and define character-specific advantages. Perk Cards are rolled with Perk Points, arranged into a deck, and equipped to a Civilian loadout. Starting items are unlocked through character progression and can then change how a match begins.

Do not treat these systems as interchangeable. Unlocking a Pocket Knife through Lynda's progression, for example, is a starting-item unlock; it is not automatically one of Lynda's signature traits or a card drawn during a match. Current players report the Pocket Knife unlock at Lynda Civilian Level 10, but IllFonic has not published a complete level-by-level starting-item table.

## Michael Myers

[Michael Myers](/characters/michael-myers) is the entire killer side of the 1v4 match. His fixed starting traits are Killer Sense and Shape Jump, and his loadout includes three additional selected abilities such as Reality Tear, Blackout, or Detection Pulse.

Michael also hunts NPC residents and assigned Special Targets. His best beginner plan is not to chase one player forever: stalk targets, control darkness, kill residents efficiently, and pressure noisy police or escape objectives. The dedicated guide includes current [Michael Myers abilities and cooldowns](/characters/michael-myers).

## The Ten Standard Civilians

![Four legacy characters in Polaroid-style portraits](/images/official/group-polaroid-vf.webp)

The official Standard roster is Jennifer, Tanya, Rachel, Eric, Marcus, Thomas, Bob, Lynda, Annie, and Laurie. Bob, Lynda, Annie, and Laurie are legacy characters reimagined from the 1978 film; the other six expand the playable Haddonfield cast.

Some Standard characters still require Profile progression before they can be selected. That does not make them paid Deluxe characters. Compare the locked character's requirement with your Profile level, not only the level of your current Civilian.

## Richard and Alexis

Richard and Alexis are the two additional playable Civilians included with the Digital Deluxe Edition. They are roster characters, not cosmetic skins. The official materials do not list a separate individual purchase price for either character.

See the [edition comparison](/editions) for current package contents. Keep the two Deluxe Civilians separate from the Final Girl Laurie cosmetic and the Inmate or Phantom Michael skins.

## NPC Residents, Police, and Dr. Loomis

NPC residents are not selectable starting Civilians. They live across Haddonfield, react to conversations, and can be told to hide, follow a player, or help contact police. Saving them is a core Civilian objective; killing them is central to Michael's score and pressure.

A dead player may later return as a Sheriff's Deputy or Dr. Loomis. These are reinforcement roles, not ordinary selections on the initial Civilian roster. The [police respawn guide](/guides/how-to-respawn-as-police) explains the officially confirmed roles and community-tested card sequence.

## Best Character for Beginners

There is no verified universal best character. Pick by task:

- Choose stronger Athleticism when you want to scout, move between residents, or use bikes.
- Choose stronger Personality when you want to warn residents, call authorities, or lead followers.
- Choose stronger Resourcefulness when you want to repair, drive, use firearms, and work escape objectives.
- Choose stronger Capability when you expect to protect teammates with melee or throwable items.

Early characters are sufficient for learning the game. A well-understood trait and Perk Deck usually matter more than grinding immediately toward the last roster unlock.
