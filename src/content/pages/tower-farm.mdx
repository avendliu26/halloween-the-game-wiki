Tower Farm is map 5, a free Halloween: The Game multiplayer map announced on October 5, 2026 for the scheduled [October 27 update](/updates/october-27-2026). It is not part of the four [launch maps](/locations).

## Confirmed setting

Tower Farm takes inspiration from Halloween 5 settings. The announcement confirms rural roads, a filling station, cornfields, roadside ditches, farmhouses, farm equipment, hay bales, brush, barns, sheds, a harvest festival, a trailer park, and pumpkin gardens.

Its open rural areas mean exposed crossings and longer sightlines than the launch neighborhoods.

## What is not established yet

Spawn behavior, routes, objective placement, map dimensions, and tested balance are not published. This page makes no claims about spawns, strategies, dimensions, or balance before the map is playable.
