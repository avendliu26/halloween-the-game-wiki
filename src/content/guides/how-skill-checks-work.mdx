## Quick Answer

Players use “skill checks” to describe the timed spectator prompts or minigames shown after a Civilian dies. Steam users report that completing them awards XP and can produce useful cards, including a chance to return as police.

IllFonic has not published the exact input windows, reward table, card odds or a complete list of activities. Treat precise percentages and guaranteed reward claims as unconfirmed.

## Official Information

The [official multiplayer overview](https://halloweengame.com/news/multiplayer-gameplay-overview/) confirms that death does not necessarily end a player's contribution. A defeated Civilian can support friends and may return as a Sheriff's Deputy or Dr. Loomis.

The [official progression overview](https://halloweengame.com/news/progression-customization-overview/) confirms that completing matches earns XP and that the game has challenge, progression and Perk Card systems. Neither article publicly explains the spectator prompts step by step.

## Community-Reported Behavior

In a [Steam discussion about remaining after death](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3219630/discussions/0/592940297913404653/), several players describe post-death minigames, XP rewards and the possibility of returning as police. A second [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3219630/discussions/0/592940297913363357/) specifically connects completing skill checks with receiving a police respawn card.

These reports agree on the broad loop but disagree about how often the return card appears. That disagreement is why this guide does not publish a drop rate.

## What to Do After Death

1. Stay in the match instead of leaving immediately.
2. Follow the prompt currently shown in the spectator interface.
3. Complete the timing or input sequence as accurately as possible.
4. Check each awarded card before using or dismissing it.
5. Use a return opportunity if the match presents one.

The on-screen prompt is the authoritative instruction for your current build. Input labels can differ by platform and control method, so a keyboard-specific sequence should not be presented as universal.

## Do Skill Checks Give XP?

Steam players report receiving XP from spectator minigames. The official progression article separately confirms that completing matches and playing story mode award XP, but it does not publish the spectator reward amount.

Do not rely on a fixed XP number from a single match. Rewards may depend on the activity, successful inputs, match state or version, and those factors are not officially documented here.

## Do Skill Checks Guarantee a Police Card?

No guaranteed rate is officially confirmed. Some Steam users report seeing the card frequently, while others describe long waits without a return. The safe conclusion is that completing prompts preserves the opportunity; it does not promise that every attempt produces the same card.

See [how to respawn as police](/guides/how-to-respawn-as-police) for the full official-versus-community distinction.

## Are Repair Prompts the Same System?

Not officially confirmed. IllFonic says Resourcefulness affects repair expertise and environmental awareness, but the public article does not call every repair interaction a skill check or state that it shares the spectator reward system.

Keep those mechanics separate unless an in-game description or official guide explicitly connects them.

## FAQ

### Should I leave after my Civilian dies?

Stay if you want XP, support activities or a possible return role. Community reports say leaving early can forfeit the remaining opportunities.

### What happens if I miss a prompt?

The exact penalty is not officially documented. Continue with the next instruction the game provides.

### Are skill-check timings identical on every platform?

Not officially confirmed. Use the prompts and controls shown by your current platform build.
