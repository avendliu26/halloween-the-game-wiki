## Launch Details

Halloween: The Game is scheduled for a digital release on September 8, 2026, on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store.

The Digital Standard Edition costs $39.99 USD; Digital Deluxe costs $59.99 USD. Eligible Digital Deluxe pre-orders include early access starting September 4 at 9 AM Pacific Time. Regional prices may differ.

## Core Match Structure

Multiplayer is asymmetrical: one Michael Myers player faces four Civilian players. The setting is Haddonfield on Halloween night, 1978. Single-player content is also confirmed.

## Michael Myers

Michael Myers stalks the town as the Boogeyman. His role is different from the cooperative Civilian side. We are not publishing unverified ability values or suggested builds.

## Civilians

Civilians work together to survive, gather useful equipment, and pursue escape objectives. The exact controls and best routes should be learned from verified in-game information, not assumed from other horror games.
