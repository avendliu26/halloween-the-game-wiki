Prepare for Halloween: The Game by checking your platform, edition and access date, then learning the difference between Michael's hunt and the Civilian rescue objectives. This checklist organizes those decisions; the linked reference pages hold the detailed facts.

It is a pre-launch preparation guide, not a tested first-match walkthrough. No control bindings, recommended builds or guaranteed escape routes are asserted here.

## Launch Details

Match the edition on your receipt with the [release schedule](/release-date). Digital launch, eligible Deluxe pre-order early access and physical release are different dates. A cosmetic described as advance access is not necessarily early access to the game.

If you have not chosen a copy, compare the [digital editions](/editions) and [physical packages](/physical-editions). Keep edition ownership separate from preorder-only bonuses when checking the product description.

## Check Your Platform

Use the [platform reference](/platforms) to find the correct store and review regional availability and console online-play subscriptions. Do not assume that a US store listing establishes availability in your country.

For PC, compare your OS, RAM, GPU and storage with the [published system requirements](/system-requirements). The CPU rows use broad socket labels, so a matching socket alone does not establish a tested performance result.

## Understand the Roles Before Choosing One

Read [how to play](/guides/how-to-play) for the actual objective loop. Four Civilians are trying to rescue NPC residents, not just escape individually, while Michael has tracking and stealth systems of his own.

The [character directory](/characters) separates the Standard roster, Deluxe additions, NPC residents and conditional return roles. Skin names are not additional characters or verified stat upgrades.

## Learn Landmarks Without Memorizing a Fake Route

The [map directory](/locations) identifies the four launch neighborhoods and their recognizable buildings. Use it as orientation, not an exact spawn chart: the developer describes changing escape opportunities and item requirements.

This is why the preparation checklist does not ask you to memorize one supposedly universal escape sequence. A source-based map overview and a tested route guide answer different questions.

## Keep Unresolved Questions Separate

Xbox and Epic list cross-platform functionality, but the reviewed sources do not provide a complete cross-network invitation or save-transfer guide. Before organizing a mixed-platform group, check for instructions that actually name the relevant platforms and features.

Likewise, no numeric character tier list or precise input sequence is supplied by this checklist. Consult the game's own current settings and instructions once you have access, then compare verified guidance against that version.
