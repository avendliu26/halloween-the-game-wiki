## Core Match Structure

Halloween: The Game features 1v4 asymmetrical multiplayer: one player becomes Michael Myers while four players take Civilian roles. Matches are set around Haddonfield in 1978.

## Michael Myers

Michael Myers is the solo hunter. Official materials describe stalking and terrorizing residents. Detailed ability descriptions, cooldowns, and tactical recommendations await verification.

## Civilians

The Civilian side cooperates to survive, scavenge equipment, and work toward escape. This wiki does not yet offer a step-by-step escape sequence or claim that one route works on every map.

## Maps and Single-Player

The four announced multiplayer maps are East Haddonfield, Haddonfield Heights, Orange Grove Estates, and Haddonfield Town Center. See the [Maps directory](/locations) for confirmed landmarks.

Single-player content is listed on Steam. Its full structure is outside the confirmed detail covered by this first-stage wiki.
