## Confirmed Facts

- Fact: One Michael faces four playable Civilians plus NPC residents; Civilians convince residents, locate loot, use changing escape opportunities and may return as deputy or Loomis. Michael has Special Targets, Killer Sense, Stalk, Shape Jump and Shape Dash. Escape activation reveals its location by noise; Michael can be detained, not killed.
  - Source: Multiplayer Gameplay Overview (published 2026-03-26)
  - URL: https://halloweengame.com/news/multiplayer-gameplay-overview/
  - Accessed date: 2026-09-03

- Fact: Singleplayer follows Michael over six chapters narrated by Dr. Loomis, with fixed core goals, sandbox approaches, optional challenges, collectibles, difficulty choices and multiplayer cosmetic unlocks.
  - Source: Unleash Hell Upon Haddonfield (published 2026-06-06)
  - URL: https://halloweengame.com/news/unleash-hell-upon-haddonfield/
  - Accessed date: 2026-09-03

- Fact: Both sides use stealth; noise, flashlights and careless escorting create exposure.
  - Source: Stealth, Stalking, and Scares (published 2025-10-01)
  - URL: https://halloweengame.com/news/stealth-stalking-and-scares/
  - Accessed date: 2026-09-03

- Fact: Four launch multiplayer maps are East Haddonfield, Heights, Orange Grove Estates and Town Center.
  - Source: The Locations of Halloween: The Game (published 2026-09-01)
  - URL: https://halloweengame.com/news/the-locations-of-halloween-the-game/
